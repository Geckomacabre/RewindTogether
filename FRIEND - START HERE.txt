===========================================================
  REWIND TOGETHER  -  co-op for Retro Rewind   (v1.7.0)
  Someone sent you this so you can play together.
===========================================================


STEP 1  -  INSTALL  (once)
--------------------------

  Unzip this folder anywhere and run:

      RewindTogetherLauncher.exe

  It finds Retro Rewind, installs what is missing (the UE4SS
  loader and this mod) and backs up your saves first.
  Nothing to edit, no IDs to swap.


STEP 2  -  PLAY
---------------

  IF YOU ARE HOSTING (it is your store):
    Press HOST MY STORE in the launcher. Pick your save in the
    game like always and walk into your store. It opens to your
    friends by itself.

  IF YOU ARE VISITING:
    In the launcher, under VISIT A FRIEND, pick the friend whose
    store is open - or use "Other ways to join" and type the
    address they give you. The game starts and connects straight
    from the main menu. You do not need a save of your own.

  Not on the same network? Either use the Steam route above
  (new - tell us if it works for you), or both join the same
  free VPN (Tailscale / ZeroTier / Radmin) and use the address
  it gives the host.


THINGS TO KNOW
--------------

  * It is the HOST's store and the HOST's save. Visiting never
    touches your own saves.

  * You can work the store: right-click picks up / uses,
    left-click places, E / V / B switch tools, K sells.

  * Both of you need the same mod version AND the same game
    version. The launcher shows both.

  * A black UE4SS window opens next to the game. That is
    normal - leave it open.


IF IT DOES NOT WORK
-------------------

  1. Launcher -> DIAGNOSTICS -> "Copy diagnostics", and send
     that to whoever gave you the mod.
  2. If the game ever crashes, just start it again. The mod
     notes what it was doing, switches that one feature off on
     the next launch and tells you which (type rt.safety in the
     black window).
