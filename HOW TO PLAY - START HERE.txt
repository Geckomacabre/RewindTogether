=============================================================
  REWIND TOGETHER  -  play Retro Rewind together   (v1.9.0)
  The simple guide. Read your part: HOST or GUEST.
=============================================================

  HOST  = the person whose store you all play in.
  GUEST = everyone who visits that store.


BEFORE YOU START  (both of you)
-------------------------------

  [ ] You both own Retro Rewind on Steam.
  [ ] You are Steam friends with each other.
  [ ] You both have the SAME zip of Rewind Together.
  [ ] Steam has finished updating Retro Rewind for both of you.


INSTALL  (both of you, once)
----------------------------

  1. Right-click the zip and choose "Extract All...".
     (Opening it straight from the zip does not work.)

  2. In the folder you just got, double-click
         RewindTogetherLauncher.exe

  3. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC":
     click "More info", then "Run anyway".

  That's it. The launcher finds the game, backs up your
  saves, and installs everything. Nothing to download, nothing
  to edit. If the game is open, it offers to close it.


UPDATING  (both of you)
-----------------------

  When a new version is out, the launcher shows a green
  banner. Press  Update . It downloads the new version,
  checks it, and restarts by itself in about a minute.
  Your saves and settings stay as they are.

  * Update when you are NOT playing together.
  * Update together: you both need the same version.


IF YOU ARE THE HOST
-------------------

  1. Open the launcher.
  2. (Optional) Under SAVE, pick which save to play.
  3. Press the big pink  HOST MY STORE  button.
  4. The game starts. Load your save if it asks, and walk into
     your store.
  5. Done - your store is now open to your Steam friends.
     The launcher shows "NOW HOSTING" and lists your GUESTS as
     they arrive. Use "Invite" to send a friend a Steam invite.

  Keep the game running while you play together. Save the way
  you always do: it is YOUR store and YOUR save.

  To stop: close the game, or press STOP in the launcher.


IF YOU ARE A GUEST
------------------

  1. Wait until your host is standing in their store.
  2. Open the launcher.
  3. On the right, under VISIT A FRIEND, press  Refresh.
  4. Your host appears under FRIENDS WITH THEIR STORE OPEN.
     Press  JOIN  next to their name.
  5. The game starts and drops you into their store.
     You do NOT need a save of your own.

  Also works: in Steam's friends list, right-click your host
  and choose "Join Game".

  Your own saves are never touched while you visit.


PLAYING TOGETHER
----------------

  Everyone can work the store, with the normal controls:

    Right-click ....... pick up / use
    Left-click ........ put down / place
    E / V / B ......... hand / build mode / paint (skin) tool
    K ................. sell
    At the till ....... Space closes the cash drawer,
                        W walks away from the register

  Guests: switch tools with E, V and B. The mouse wheel does
  not switch tools for guests yet.

  Anyone can order movies at the office computer (the Market,
  New and Black Market pages) and look things up on the Data
  page. It is the store's money, and the tapes arrive at the
  delivery spot for everyone.

  Guests can also use the catalogue (shelves, equipment,
  decorations and the counter stations), the store expansion
  map, the sidewalk sign, the store's name sign, the snack
  shelves, the popcorn, slushie and cotton candy machines, the
  repair desk (a broken tape is repaired on your own screen) and
  the console rental (mat, monitor and shelf).

  YOUR CHARACTER: the first time you are in a store (your own
  save or a friend's), the character screen opens by itself,
  on the character you made last. Pick one of the store's
  regulars, or make your own: body, skin, hair and its colour,
  beard, glasses, top, bottom and shoes with their colours,
  and height. RANDOM makes a new one, DONE keeps it for this
  store, Esc or CANCEL keeps the one you have on. Each store
  remembers its own. Change it any time with Esc >
  "Character", or F4. The others see you that way.

  The HOST decides when the day ends. Guests see the day's
  summary too and carry on into the next day.

  Press F6 any time to see if you are connected and in sync.

  Press Esc for the pause menu (guests too). Its "Back to the
  store" button gets you out if you are ever stuck.

  If Discord is open, your profile says "Playing Rewind
  Together" while you play. To turn that off: launcher,
  DIAGNOSTICS, switch off "Show 'Playing Rewind Together'".


GETTING MORE MODS (optional)
----------------------------

  1. In the launcher: MODS, then NEXUS MODS.
  2. Find a mod and press Download.
  3. Its page opens on Nexus Mods. Click the download button
     there (with a free Nexus account: "Slow download").
  4. Done. The launcher notices the file in your Downloads
     folder and installs it by itself.

  If you play together, the host and every guest should have
  the same content mods.


IF IT DOES NOT WORK
-------------------

  My host is not in the list
    - The host must be INSIDE their store, not at the menu.
    - Press Refresh again after a few seconds.
    - You must be Steam friends.
    - Check you both have the same mod version: the launcher's
      PLAY tab shows it at the top.

  It still will not connect over Steam
    Use "Other ways to join (IP address, Steam ID)":
    - JOIN BY STEAM ID: the host's ID is shown on their
      launcher under INVITE A FRIEND.
    - JOIN BY ADDRESS: install the free VPN Tailscale on both
      PCs, sign in to the same Tailscale network, and type the
      host's Tailscale address.

  The launcher says your mods are different
    Before you join, the launcher compares your mods with the
    host's and says what differs:
    - A different Rewind Together version: whoever is behind
      presses Update first.
    - Different content mods (.pak or Blueprint mods): get the
      same files the host has, from wherever they got them.
      Otherwise some things may be missing from the store.
    - Different script mods: usually fine, they run on each
      PC separately.
    If the host got their mods through the launcher, press
    "Get their mods": it lists exactly those, with Download.
    "Join anyway" still works if you want to try.

  Steam updated the game for only one of you
    You both need the same game version. Let Steam finish
    updating on both PCs.

  Stuck outside the store, in a wall, or somewhere odd
    Press Esc and choose "Back to the store". A guest lands
    next to the host; the host lands by the register.

  The game crashed
    Just start it again. The mod remembers what it was doing,
    turns that one feature off, and keeps going.

  Anything else
    In the launcher: DIAGNOSTICS > "Copy diagnostics".
    Paste that to whoever sent you the mod. If you can, do it
    on BOTH PCs (host and guest).


  The Extras folder is only for playing without Steam (opening
  a port on your router). Most people never need it.
